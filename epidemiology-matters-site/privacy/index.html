Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

Epidemiology Matters is a teaching companion. The interactive tools on this site — the Causal Thinking Lab, Farrlandia, and the homepage story — run entirely in your browser. The data you explore is generated locally on your own device and is never sent to us.

Email sign-ups

If you join our launch list, we store only the email address you provide, and we use it only to tell you when the second edition and its tools become available. We do not sell or share it. You can ask to be removed at any time by replying to any message we send.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand which pages are useful. These do not identify individual visitors.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be directed to the authors through their institutional pages linked on the home page.
