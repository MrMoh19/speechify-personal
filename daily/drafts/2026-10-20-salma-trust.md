I know we have talked through a few analyses, and I have not sent you any of them yet, so I am taking the initiative with this one.

Trust is a question I had thought about before, but your presentation comparing India with the other countries got me to dig deeper. Using the Wellcome Global Monitor, I looked at what the trust questions are actually measuring across countries. The short answer is that people seem to hold two closely linked kinds of trust, one in science and scientists and one in the people around them, and both help predict views on vaccines and confidence in getting healthcare. One caution came out of it: the questions do not mean quite the same thing from country to country, so comparing average trust between countries needs care. For this I used some of the methods I have been working with lately, factor analysis to find the underlying constructs and machine learning to test whether they hold in countries the model has not seen.

I attach [the figures and a short summary]. I would love your thoughts on whether it is worth taking further and how it might connect with your multi-country work.
